| 5 | `permute_contrasts_one` (13c), `PERMS`, `raw_trans_null_row` (functions_power.R) | Raw (difference) contrasts of phi have a bootstrap estimate and SE (`rdisp_*` in `BURST.CONTRASTS`) but no permutation p-value. A validation study of the trans null (`raw_trans_null_inputs.R`, `raw_trans_null_grid.R`, `raw_trans_null_summary.R`) scores the pooled relabelings and a recentered bootstrap on both scales. Local pilot (200 cells per group, 400 datasets, 400 draws, 8 scenarios; not the cluster grid): on the raw scale all three nulls held their type I error (0.01 to 0.065 at alpha 0.05, including a 4x hybrid mean) and had similar power.
