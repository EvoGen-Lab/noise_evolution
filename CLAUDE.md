# Yeast transcriptional noise pipeline

Reproducible R pipeline for genome-wide noise evolution in S. cerevisiae (YPS1000), S. eubayanus, and their F1 hybrid (10x scRNA-seq, allele-specific). Scientific context and settled decisions live in @docs/project_context.md. Read it before changing any analysis logic.

## Files
- `analysis/analysis.R` main analysis, organized in numbered sections (1, 2.1, 2.3, 4, 6.1 ... 8).
- `R/setup.R` session setup shared by `analysis.R`, `power_analysis.R` and `external_validation.R`: renv check, project paths and output folders, class names, color palettes, and `R/functions.R`. Each script starts with `library(here); source(here("R", "setup.R"))`. Defines no functions.
- `R/functions.R` helper functions sourced by the main script.
- `analysis/power_analysis.R` Section 10 (power analysis) with its functions in `R/functions_power.R`, also sourced by `power_grid.R`. Sections 10.1 to 10.8 simulate the total contrast; 10.9 to 10.14 simulate cis and trans on paired-allele data (`power_modes_grid.R`, `power_modes.sub`). Reads `PR`, `N.BOOT` and the real design (hybrid and parent cell counts, `SPLIT.FRAC`) from the Section 2 checkpoint. The simulations test both axes as the pipeline does (`sign_boot_p()` on bootstrap replicates; a dataset with a zero or failed observed fit is untested and counts as not called).
- `analysis/external_validation.R` Section 9 (comparison with published data) with its functions in `R/functions_external.R`. Reads `GENES` and `CONTRAST.FITS` from the Section 2 checkpoint.
- `analysis/checks/` validation checks of the methods document (`docs/methods.md`), separate from the main analysis: `checks_sim.R` (simulation checks A1, A2, A4, A7, B3, B4, C4, C7, E3, F1 to F4; the checks of the retired permutation and shifted bootstrap nulls and likelihood bounds, D1 to D7, D10, E1, H5, and of the retired transcriptome share correction, B2 (simulation arm) and B5, are gone with them; B3 and F2 check the library split correction), `checks_data.R` (data checks from the Section 1 and 2 checkpoints and a local bootstrap of a gene subsample) `checks_text.R` (G2 DOIs through Crossref, G3 equations, `REPORT.md`) `estimator_leak.R` (Cox-Reid against the Appendix A moment estimator by mean count and phi, and the leak test of mean differences into dispersion contrasts; needs only the Section 1 checkpoint) and `artefact_tests.R` (tests T0 to T12 of why the data depart from the NB, with functions in `R/functions_artefacts.R` and the simulation array `artefact_null_grid.R`, `artefact_null.sub`; needs only the Section 1 checkpoint) and `estimator_mse.R` (M1: bias, variance and MSE with their SEs of the Cox-Reid and Appendix A dispersion estimators over the data's mu and phi ranges on the observed depths, for gamma, lognormal and bimodal expression factors; M2: the same for the log2 total, cis and trans contrasts under null, cis-effect and trans-effect designs, with allele pairs sharing an extrinsic factor; M3: the false-positive rate of the pipeline's dispersion test (bootstrap sign test) when a rare cell state occurs at the same frequency in every group; `MSE.RUN` picks the parts; needs only the Section 1 checkpoint) and `state_conditioned.R` (SC1 to SC3: each gene's Cox-Reid dispersion with and without continuous cell-state scores from GO modules in the mean, the gene left out of its own module, and the cis and trans contrasts and own slopes under both; needs only the Section 1 checkpoint) and `sign_boot_test.R` (P1: type I error and power of the bootstrap sign test for every dispersion-family contrast, phi, theta, zeta and kappa on both scales, for Poisson and small equal dispersions at the observed depths and 1000 or 2000 cells, with the cost of counting ties and undefined replicates on the null side; `sign_boot_cell()`, with `sign_boot_p()` in `R/functions.R`; the full grid runs as `sign_boot_grid.R`, `sign_boot.sub`; blocks INPUTS, LOCAL, MERGE) and `mid_shift_check.R` (MID1: the median midparent contrast d of the log2 mean and log2 dispersion over calibrator genes with non-significant trans and total contrasts, with gene-bootstrap and cell-bootstrap intervals (`mid_shift_gene()`), against the typical significant d; the size of a shared culture and ploidy shift; needs only the Section 2 checkpoint); `mid_shift_ckpt1.R` gives its point estimates from the Section 1 checkpoint alone, with calibrators chosen by |total| and |trans| below a threshold tau; `mid_excess_ckpt1.R` (MID2) splits each gene's variance in each group into a part linear in the mean and the dispersion (`linear_excess_gene()`, Var = (1 + a) M + phi M^2) and recomputes d from the quadratic part, to tell a counting excess in the hybrid from a true rise of phi (Section 1 checkpoint only); `mid_thin_ckpt1.R` (MID3) binomially thins each parent's UMIs to its allele's depth and recomputes the dispersion d, to tell a depth effect from a truly noisier hybrid (Section 1 checkpoint only); `mid_subsample_ckpt1.R` (MID4) adds the subsampling of the hybrid cells to each parent's cell count, so depth and cell number are both matched (Section 1 checkpoint only); `dom_shift_bins_ckpt1.R` (MID5) plots the dominance contrasts of log2 phi against log2 mu_H with equal-count decile medians, half-decile medians and a loess fit, flagging deciles whose shift slopes (Section 1 checkpoint only) and `state_groups.R` (ST1: every cell labelled on three state axes, metabolism with 3 levels, cell-cycle phase with 4 and stress with 2, from 10 marker genes per level (the first present of ordered candidates), scored by a depth-aware z (`module_z()`) and assigned by `state_assign()`, the cell numbers in all 24 states per group and a PCA per group without the marker genes, with each PC's correlation with depth and its top loadings; needs only the Section 1 checkpoint). Their functions, including the shared generator `sim_hybrid_dataset()`, are in `R/functions_checks.R`. Results go to `results/checks/` (one `<ID>.rds` each and `summary.csv`); `docs/validation_checks.md` records the code and document mismatches and the latest numbers.
- Cluster scripts, each a standalone R script plus a `.sub` SLURM file. `gene_cond.R` (`gene_cond.sub`, array over gene blocks, `N.COND.ARRAY`) runs the state-conditioned layer of Section 2.5.

## Environment
- Local: RStudio on Windows. Cluster: Negishi (RCAC), account `bphm`, partition `cpu`, module `r/4.5.2`.
- Both environments are in R 4.5.x with Seurat v5. Use one shared dated Posit Package Manager CRAN snapshot for both.
- Section 2 of `analysis/analysis.R` computes offsets as direct library-size factors (no scran; Methods: Library split correction): a parent's offset is its library over the median parental library D, each hybrid allele is offset by the library of its own genome, L_Hc / D or L_He / D, and the summed hybrid by L_H / D. Allele means are per-genome rates with no rescaling, and the share s enters no contrast. Section 1.2 reports the share (`S.HAT` = L_Hc / L_H per cell, `S.BAR`, `SHARE.BOOT`, `SHARE.TABLE`) and the split variances of every hybrid cell (`SPLIT.ETA` = `split_eta()`: eta_Hc = (1 - s) / (s L_H), eta_He = s / ((1 - s) L_H), eta_x). Every allele fit subtracts the split bias delta = sum eta M^2 / F^2 / (2 sum I) from phi* (`fit_dispersion(eta)`, I the per-cell information from `nb_cell_info()`, gridded on (M, phi) and interpolated), before truncation at zero; each dataset carries the split variances of its cells (`SPLIT.FIT.ETAS`, `CONTRAST.ETAS`; 0 for parents and `HYB.COMB`), and each bootstrap replicate reads those of its resampled cells. Section 1.2 sizes the split bias against `SHARE.TOL` times the median allele phi* (`SHARE.DISP`); Section 2.2 tabulates the share of each subset (`SPLIT.OFFSET`). The summed counts are fitted without a correction, for reporting only.
- Claude Code cannot reach the cluster. Write the scripts and `.sub` files, never run `sbatch` or assume cluster output exists.

Cluster-dependent sections are 2.1 (pilot), 2.3 (bootstrap and permutation), 2.5 (state-conditioned layer, `gene_cond.R`), 4 (coexpression), 6.1 (NuPoP), and 10 (power analysis).

## Architecture rules
- Cluster pattern: the main analysis packages inputs locally, saves an `.rda`, the cluster script computes, and the main analysis loads the output and verifies that keys match. Keep new cluster work in this pattern.
- NuPoP runs only on the Linux cluster. Its compiled Fortran aborts the hosting R process on Windows regardless of input.
- Bootstrap resampling draws all indices before any Seurat call (`make_boot_ky()`). `RunPCA()` reseeds the global RNG, so a single `set.seed()` before a loop collapses the draws. `collapse_score()` should stay near 0 (0.01 confirmed).
- Fix architectures, not individual crash triggers.
- Checkpoints follow `sectionN_checkpoint.rda`. If a refactor adds an object that downstream sections need, add it to the checkpoint

## Conventions
- R files use Windows CRLF line endings. `.sub` SLURM files use Unix LF line endings. `.gitattributes` enforces both. Keep it intact.
- Each section of an analysis script starts with `set.seed(SEED.SECTION + n)` (`SEED.SECTION` is defined in `R/setup.R`). A step that needs its own reproducible stream draws it through `with_local_seed(seed, expr)`, which restores the caller's random state.
- Check scalar conditions. Use `&&` and `||` only with length-one logical values. A parse-based scan of every `&&` and `||` in `R/`, `analysis/` and `cluster/scripts/` found only scalar operands.
- Write a function only when its code runs more than once: it is called from two or more places, or it is applied across many items (`lapply`, `sapply`, `vapply`, `Map`, `mapply`, `apply`, `parLapply`, `mclapply`). Code that runs once is written inline where it runs, so a reader follows the steps in order.
  - `analysis/analysis.R`, `analysis/power_analysis.R`, `analysis/external_validation.R`, `R/setup.R` and the cluster scripts define no named functions. A function that is applied across items, or called from two or more places, lives in `R/functions.R`, and is passed to the apply call with its other inputs as named arguments (`lapply(X, name, arg = value)`). A short one-line anonymous function in an apply call (`sapply(x, function(g) cor(a[g], b[g]))`) is fine inline and should not become a separate function. A multi-line body belongs in a named function in `R/functions.R`. Use `[[` for plain accessors.
  - A once-used step in `analysis.R` or a cluster script is written inline (a `local({ ... })` block when it needs private variables), with its concept comment above it.
  - A function that PSOCK workers need by name must be a top-level function in `R/functions.R` (`neg_binom_fit_offset`, `.fit_one`); `fit_counts_offset()` finds such dependencies from the code.
  - Before adding a function, check that it will run more than once; inline it if not.
- Noise axis: `DISP` is the dispersion phi (Var = mu + phi mu^2); theta = 1 / phi is the NB size and behaves as burst frequency, and `.fit_one()` returns both. The mean is the ratio estimate sum(y) / sum(offset), free of phi, and phi maximizes the Cox-Reid adjusted profile likelihood over log(phi) (`.cr_loglik()`; phi from about 3e-7 to 55, fits at either end are recorded as `HIT_RANGE`), with phi = 0 when the adjusted slope at zero is not positive. There is no `PHI.FLOOR`; truncation at zero comes after the split correction. Testing (Methods: Tests of contrasts, Multiple testing): every per-gene contrast is tested by one bootstrap sign test (`sign_boot_p()`), computed in `boot_contrasts_one()` beside each estimate and interval. Each replicate resamples each parent's cells and the undivided hybrid cells once, each copy keeping its cis, trans and partition memberships (`make_draws()`), refits every group with the observed estimators and the split variances of its resampled cells. A failed refit counts as a zero dispersion. On the log2 scale a zero mean or dispersion is +-Inf by the sign of the group's weight lambda_r (NaN, undefined, when both signs meet); raw theta and kappa are infinite at phi = 0. n0 counts the replicates on the null side of zero (at or below for a positive estimate, at or above for a negative one), ties and undefined replicates included, and p = min(1, 2 (1 + n0) / (1 + B)), B = `N.BOOT`. There are no permutation, simulated or shifted bootstrap nulls, no resolution gate or zero-fraction bookkeeping, and no likelihood-bound comparison (`dispersion_bound_stats()` survives in `R/functions_artefacts.R` for the artefact tests only). Observed-fit rules (definition flags of `GENE.TABLE`, `GATES`, `apply_gate()`, `.QUANTITY.GATE`): a failed observed fit leaves no estimate, interval or p-value on any scale; a zero observed estimate leaves none on log2 and none for raw theta or kappa, while raw phi and raw beta proceed. Flags: `mean_<mode>_valid` informative means; `raw_<mode>_valid` phi >= 0 and `log_<mode>_valid` phi > 0 in the noise source (`rtheta` reads the log flag); `bsize_<mode>_valid`, `rbsize_<mode>_valid` (phi >= 0) and `kbal_<mode>_valid` (also raw kappa) in their own sources. Every defined NB contrast gets a p-value. Quantities with p-values: `mean` (log2), `disp` (log2 phi), `rdisp` (raw phi), `rtheta` (raw theta, noise partition), `bsize`/`rbsize` (burst size beta = mu phi), `kbal`/`rkbal` (kappa = 1 / (mu phi^2)); log2 theta = -log2 phi reuses the disp test, and for total and cis the log2 and raw dispersion tests give the same p-value. Raw hybrid values are averages of the allele values (theta_H, beta_H, kappa_H), and raw contrasts are sum_r lambda_r x_r (`.contrast_burst_raw()`). Intervals are percentile intervals that keep +-Inf replicates and may be one-sided or unbounded; the SE is the interquantile range / 3.92 (`.boot_summary()`). BH (`PR`, `_q`) runs over every contrast (the parental and inheritance contrasts and the shifted contrasts included) and quantity, one family per `_p` column over the genes that have a p-value. The weighted method-of-moments columns (`w*`, Methods Appendix C) carry estimates and intervals only and are not tested; `w<q>_<mode>_flag` marks a moment estimate outside the Cox-Reid 95% percentile interval, and the scatters draw such genes as open squares (`moment_flag_vec()`, `STATE.PCH`). `GENES` is the matched set (`in_matched_set`: an informative mean and phi > 0 in every contrast dataset, so every contrast is defined) that the main-text comparisons and downstream log2 analyses use; the supplementary tables use each per-contrast set (`GENE.SET.SIZES`, `EXCLUDED.COUNTS`). The jobs run on the inference set `GENES.INFER` (every gene with at least one defined contrast). The hybrid cells are split four times by depth, at `SPLIT.FRAC$f_mean` (mean), `f_disp` (noise), `f_kbal` (kinetic balance) and `f_bsize` (burst size); the last three pool gamma over the pilot genes with no zero or failed replicate (`PILOT.RESOLVED`), and kinetic balance and burst size fit mean and dispersion on the same cells, giving 21 datasets (`.DRAW.KEY`); the summed counts `HYB.COMB` (phi_sum, Appendix D) and the allele fits of all hybrid cells (`HYB.SC`, `HYB.SE`, which give log2 mu_H) are fitted for reporting only and enter no contrast (`.CONTRAST.GROUPS`, 18 datasets). `fit_dispersion(method = "NB" or "weighted")` is the shared fitting interface (`.fit_one()` is its NB wrapper). Every contrast is a weighted sum over its groups (`.MODE.WEIGHTS`, lambda of +-1 or +-1/2). Means are log2 x NB only. Modes (`.OUT_MODES`): total, cis, trans, mid (Delta_dom, the per-genome hybrid against the midparent), par_sc and par_se (Delta_H,Pc and Delta_H,Pe) and inh_c and inh_e (the allelic inheritance contrasts, each hybrid allele against its parent); cis uses the cis cells of each quantity's partition and every other mode on the hybrid alleles its trans cells (`.MODE.TEMPLATE`, suffixes _n, _k, _z for the noise, kinetic balance and burst size partitions), so nothing built on the hybrid alleles shares cells with cis. Every midpoint (per-genome hybrid and midparent) is taken on the scale of its contrast: geometric means on the log2 scale, for the mean as for the dispersion, and arithmetic means on the raw scale. Shared shift (Methods: Shared shift; Section 2.3) in two bootstrap stages: stage 1 (`gene_boot.R`, job array) gives the unshifted contrasts and, in replicate 1, the replicate values of `disp_mid` (`BOOT.DOM.DRAWS`); locally, Xi is one robust loess of `disp_mid` on log2 mu_H (`log2_mu_H`, geometric mean of the allele fits on all hybrid cells) over calibrators (`DOM.ADJ.CAL`: stage-1 trans and total log2 dispersion q >= `DOM.ADJ.SIG`), span `DOM.ADJ.SPAN` (`dom_shift_curve()`), refitted in every replicate on the fixed calibrators (`DOM.SHIFT`, genes x (1 + B), saved to `gene_shift_inputs.rda`); stage 2 (`GENE_BOOT_STAGE=2`, `gene_boot_shift.sub`) reruns replicate 1 with every hybrid-allele phi divided by 2^Xi (`boot_contrasts_one(shift = )`), which moves the log2 contrasts of the hybrid with the parents (-Xi for disp and bsize, +2 Xi for kbal) and scales the hybrid values of every raw contrast that uses the hybrid, giving `a<q>_<mode>_*` for the modes it moves (`.SHIFT.QUANTITIES`, `dom_col()`) and the degrees of dominance `k_<q>_*` (2 Delta_dom / Delta_total) and `kt_<q>_*` (2 Delta_dom / Delta_trans) with their bootstrap distribution at -1, 0 and 1 (`.ratio_cdf()`); its unshifted columns must equal stage 1, which analysis.R checks. The mean carries no shift. `DOM.SHIFT.DECILES` reports Xi by decile of log2 mu_H. Inheritance (`classify_dom()`, `dom_class_vec(ref = "total" or "trans")`, `DOM.VEC`, `TDOM.VEC`): reference and Delta_dom both not significant Conserved, reference only Additive, Delta_dom only Over- or Underdominant by its sign, both placed by the percentile interval of k at level 1 - 0.05 N_sel / N_test (false coverage-statement rate, Benjamini and Yekutieli 2005, https://doi.org/10.1198/016214504000001907) into Sc.Dominant, Se.Dominant, Sc.Partial, Se.Partial, Over- or Underdominant (`DOM.CLASS`; partially dominant classes share the colour of the dominant class toward the same parent); an interval within (-1, 1) containing 0 only is Additive, a case the Methods do not name. The ploidy shifts Lambda and Lambda_raw and the other Appendix D quantities are reported only (`APPENDIX.D`, `ROUTE.CHECK`). Burst size `bsize` = mean + disp with both from the burst size partition for cis and trans (`.BSIZE_SOURCE`); kinetic balance `kbal` = -mean - 2 disp with both from the kinetic balance partition (`.KBAL_SOURCE`). State conditioning (Section 2.5, Methods Appendix E): the parents share one culture and the hybrid another, so trans, mid and par carry the culture difference. Every NB contrast (mean, disp, rdisp, rtheta, bsize, kbal, rbsize, rkbal; all modes) is refitted by `cond_contrasts_one()` with the program score S (20 derepression and stress markers, the set G, `STATE.MEMBERS`; `state_scores()`: log2 marker count per offset, gene left out, centred at the median of each parent and of the hybrid cells) in the NB mean (`fit_dispersion(z = )`: Poisson score equations for the mean, mu at S = 0, slope tau as `cslope_<dataset>`, Cox-Reid adjusted for both mean parameters), on the draws of bootstrap replicate 1 with the unconditioned refit beside it, giving `c<q>_<mode>_*` (conditioned estimate, SE, interval) and `d<q>_<mode>_*` (paired change) in `COND.CONTRASTS`, gated by `.COND.GATE`. No conditioned tests. A significant call (q < `STATE.FLAG.SIG`) whose conditioned interval contains zero and whose change interval excludes zero gets `stateflag_<q>_<mode>` in `BURST.CONTRASTS`; `state_flag_vec()` reads them and the scatter functions draw flagged genes as open triangles (`STATE.PCH`). The unconditioned estimates stay the main analysis. CV2 and the Fano factor are reported per gene and group in `GENE.TABLE` (`.cv2`, `.fano`) and in the comparison with published data (Section 9); they enter no contrast or test. Every classification is per comparison: `classify_reg()` needs the cis and trans p-values, `classify_dom()` the reference contrast and the shifted Delta_dom, and a gene without them is classified NA (distinct from Ambiguous), so the classified genes differ by quantity (`CLASSIFIED.COUNTS`). `CONTRAST.FITS`, `CONTRAST.MATS` and `PLOIDY.SHIFT` stay on `GENES`, which the sections that need every dataset use. `GENE.TABLE` also keeps the fit status (NB and weighted) and `ANALYSIS.STATUS` records for every gene, quantity and mode whether it was eligible, run, estimated, given a bootstrap interval, tested and significant (`ANALYSIS.COUNTS` tallies them).
- Select Seurat columns by name, never by position (Seurat v5 changes `FindMarkers` output order).
- Validate numeric inputs at function entry (for example `BSIZE` in `SPLIT.FITS`).
